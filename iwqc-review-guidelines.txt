IWQC is a workshop whose proceeding will not be published.  Our aim is
a to put together an interesting programme of talks that reflect the
state of the art in quantum compilation and will provoke discussion.
With this in mind, please observe the following guidelines when
reviewing.  

Filter : 
Scope — if it’s not about compiling quantum software, loosely speaking, it’s not in scope.
Shite — is it obvious garbage?  Then reject and move on.

Identify : what is the main contribution of the paper?  

Judge : 
— Is it novel?  Is it likely to lead to new things?  Applied a new
  approach to an old problem?  Attacks a problem not much considered
  before?   Is it interesting? Papers which are incremental and/or
  unsurprising can still make the cut.
— Is it (probably) right?  We don’t need to check all the the proofs
  but the methods should be adequate to obtain the results presented.
  This is a workshop — we are more willing to be wrong than be
  boring. 

We don't care about : 
— is the paper well written?  This is just for talks, we won’t be
  publishing the paper.  Of course if you can't understand it then
  that is bad.

Of course, as always you can give the authors whatever feedback you
think would useful to them, but the points above are what the PC needs
to know.

Interpretation of scores :

 3 : Excellent work, this is an essential talk at the workshop.
 2 : I like it, I would go to the talk.
 1 : Mildly interesting, but skippable.
 0 : I don't care about this, but it's  probably not wrong.
-1 : This has problems, potentially fixable.
-2 : Major issues, can't be accepted.
-3 : This is wrong, fundamentally misguided, or out of scope, and must
     not be included in any form at the workshop.

Note that the authors are allowed to submit contributions of any
length, including short abstracts.  If the authors haven't given you
enough to judge the merit of their submission, then take your best
guess and use the "confidence" score to reflect your uncertainty.  You
should also make a comment to this effect in the review.

Note also anything which is in scope and not wrong will be invited for
a poster.
